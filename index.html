Dinner Party

Cook for any number of people. Keep every recipe you love.

Dinner Party is a recipe scaler and cookbook that lives on your phone. Change the servings and every amount follows, in the fractions you actually use in a kitchen.

A look inside

What it does

Scale anything

Pick a serving count, or tap half, double or triple. Every amount updates, and you can see what it was underneath.

Kitchen fractions

1 1/2 cups, not 1.5. Switch to decimals if you prefer.

I only have this much

Tell it how much of one ingredient you have, and the rest of the recipe scales to match.

Bring your own recipes

Paste or type a recipe, or bring in a PDF. Each line shows how it was read, so you can fix anything before it is saved.

Starter recipes

A catalog of everyday basics. Add any to your cookbook and make it your own.

Menus and meal prep

Plan one dinner or a week of meals. Each recipe is scaled to cover everyone.

Cooking order

Pick when you want to eat and get a suggested start time for each dish.

Share and keep

Share a menu card, or turn your recipes into a PDF cookbook with a cover and contents.

Light and evening

A printed-cookbook look that follows your phone, in warm paper or charcoal.

Swaps cover the obvious ingredients. Always read labels and check for cross-contact, especially with allergies.

Your recipes and settings are stored only on your phone. Read the privacy policy, or write to support@pieceoftech.app.
